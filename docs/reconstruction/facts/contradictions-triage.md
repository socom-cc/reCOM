## Scan defects

1. **`_reads` (batch_apply.py) reads rs for every SPECIAL instruction.** The shift-immediate
   instructions (funct 0x00/02/03/38/3A/3B/3C/3E/3F) read only rt. The rs field is reserved, and
   `mfhi`/`mflo` read nothing. Executed inline data (the `_copyRefImage` mask) therefore produces a
   phantom a3 read. `recon facts` and the entry-block check in `recon validate` share this decoder.
2. **Any-path "registers" reads cannot be exempted.** The scan drops only `dead_entry_reads`, and
   `recon validate` accepts that row only when the read is in the entry block. As a result, 7 of the
   10 false positives cannot be silenced: the dead merges in `__divdi3`, `__moddi3`, `__umoddi3` and
   `_ulp`, and the uninitialised `k` in `__ieee754_expf`. Two fixes would work, together or alone:
   - recognise the merge idiom in `argument_reads`: a keep-half `and` whose surviving half is
     masked off by the complementary `and` before any full-width read;
   - accept reviewed any-path `dead_reads` rows and honour them in the scan.
3. **The `_RECORDED` regex matches provenance remarks.** The phrases "no evidence in the
   workspace" (`kill`) and "library type archive" (`open`) say a signature is unproven, not that it
   is disproved.
4. **The default-signature test requires no convention.** It flags the repair placeholder
   `undefined __stdcall NAME(void)` (0x20e7a0; also 0x20e790/0x140500/0x140510 if they come to read
   registers). The scan should treat it as claim-free like the true default.
5. **Thunks are compared against their mangled names.** An 8-byte `j`/`b` thunk shows its
   target's signature, so a mangled count or type check on it cannot be repaired. The scan should
   report it as a thunk note, not a contradiction.
